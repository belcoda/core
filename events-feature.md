# Events: Organize, Mobilize, and Grow Your Community

Plan and execute successful community events with powerful tools built for organizers who need to move fast and stay connected with their members.

## Why Events Matter

Events are the heartbeat of community organizing. Whether you're hosting rallies, town halls, canvassing sessions, or educational workshops, bringing people together in person (or online) builds the relationships and momentum that drive real change.

## Core Features

### Flexible Event Creation

Create events that match your organizing needs:

- **In-Person, Online, or Hybrid**: Host physical events with full address support, virtual events with meeting links and instructions, or both
- **Smart Scheduling**: Set event times with automatic timezone support for accurate display no matter where your members are located
- **Capacity Management**: Set maximum attendee limits to ensure you don't overbook your venue
- **Rich Event Pages**: Add headings, detailed descriptions with formatted content, and feature images to make your events compelling
- **Custom Branding**: Upload feature images and add custom code or meta tags for social media sharing

### Multi-Channel Registration

Meet your members where they are:

- **Public Website Registration**: Beautiful, customizable event pages that make signing up simple
- **WhatsApp Registration**: Generate WhatsApp links for one-tap registration through messaging
- **Manual Registration**: Admin tools to register members directly
- **API Integration**: Connect with other tools and platforms
- **Bulk Import**: Bring in registrations from external sources

### Smart Data Collection

Gather exactly the information you need:

- **Flexible Forms**: Configure which fields to ask for (email, phone, address, postcode)
- **Required vs. Optional**: Make specific fields mandatory or optional based on your needs
- **Opt-In Tracking**: Built-in consent management for mailing lists and communications
- **Auto-Create Contacts**: New registrants automatically become contacts in your CRM with all their information

### Attendee Management

Track participation and engagement:

- **Four Status Types**: Registered, Attended, Cancelled, No-Show
- **Individual Notes**: Add notes to specific attendees for follow-up
- **Notification Control**: Toggle email notifications per attendee
- **Check-In Tools**: Mark attendance during your event
- **Quick Registration**: Register existing contacts with a few clicks
- **Printable Lists**: Export attendee lists for check-in tables

### Automated Communications

Stay connected without the manual work:

#### Registration Confirmations
Instantly send personalized confirmation emails when someone registers

#### Smart Reminders
- Automatically remind attendees before your event starts
- Customize how many hours in advance (default: 24 hours)
- One-time send with tracking to prevent duplicates

#### Follow-Up Emails
- Automatically reach out after events conclude
- Customize timing (default: 24 hours after event ends)
- Use custom email templates for targeted messaging
- Perfect for thanking attendees, sharing resources, or recruiting for the next action

#### Cancellation Notifications
Automatically notify members when they cancel their registration

### Event Organization

Keep your events organized as your work scales:

- **Tag System**: Categorize events with tags for easy filtering and reporting
- **Point Person**: Assign a team member responsible for each event
- **Activity Tracking**: Every registration, attendance, and email is logged in your activity feed
- **Soft Delete**: Archive events without losing historical data

### Integration with Your CRM

Events work seamlessly with the rest of your organizing tools:

#### People Integration
- Event registrations and attendance appear on contact profiles
- Full activity history: who registered, attended, or cancelled
- Smart filters to find people who haven't registered yet
- Automatic profile updates from registration forms

#### Communications Hub
- All event emails tracked in your communications system
- Reference email interactions in contact histories
- Use custom email templates for consistent messaging
- Multi-channel support: email, WhatsApp, SMS

#### Activity Timeline
Every event-related action creates a trackable interaction:
- Registered for event
- Attended event
- Cancelled registration
- Marked as no-show
- Received confirmation email
- Received reminder email
- Received follow-up email

### Analytics and Insights

Track your impact:
- Registration counts by status (registered, attended, cancelled, no-show)
- Signup channel tracking (website, WhatsApp, manual, import, API)
- Timestamp data for registration trends
- Integration with broader CRM reporting

## Real-World Use Cases

### Town Hall Meetings
Create an event, share the public registration link on social media, and watch registrations come in. Send automatic reminders the day before, then mark attendance during the event. Follow up with attendees afterward with a thank you and next steps.

### Canvassing Sessions
Set capacity limits for your canvassing shifts, register volunteers via WhatsApp, and track who actually showed up vs. who registered. Use attendance data to identify your most reliable volunteers.

### Virtual Training Workshops
Host online events with meeting links and instructions. Collect email addresses during registration, send reminder emails automatically, and follow up with resources and recordings after the session.

### Hybrid Community Forums
Offer both in-person and online attendance options. Track registration separately for each format, send appropriate joining instructions based on how people registered, and maintain unified attendance records.

### Rally or Action Planning
Create events for public actions, use tags to organize by campaign, assign point people to coordinate, and use the attendee list for day-of logistics and follow-up organizing.
